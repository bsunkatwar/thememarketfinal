Extend or Renew Item Support

When you purchase a template, theme, or digital product from TezTecch, the item usually comes with a limited period of item support. During this period, the author can help you with technical issues, bug fixes, and guidance on how to use the product.

If your support period has expired, you can extend or renew the support to continue receiving assistance from the author.

How to Extend or Renew Item Support

  • Log in to your TezTecch account.
  • Go to the Purchases or Downloads section.
  • Find the product for which support has expired.
  • Click the Extend Support or Renew Support option.
  • Complete the payment process to activate the extended support.

Benefits of Renewing Item Support

  • Get help with technical issues
  • Receive assistance with product features
  • Access guidance from the product author
  • Ensure your product works correctly

Once the support is renewed, you will be able to contact the author again through the TezTecch Support Center and receive help for your purchased item.
