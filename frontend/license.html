Licenses Overview

Did you know that when you purchase an item from Teztecch Market, you are purchasing a license to use that item? Understanding licenses helps you use digital assets correctly.

Standard License

The standard license allows you to use the item in one end product for yourself or one client.

Music License

Music items can be used in videos, advertisements, games, and creative productions depending on the license.

Sound Effects License

Sound effects can be used in games, films, and media productions.

Video License

Video templates and footage can be used in creative projects like marketing videos and films.

Photo License

Photos may be used for websites, advertisements, and marketing materials.

Specialty License

Specialty licenses apply to specific creative assets with custom usage rights.

FAQ

What does a license mean?

A license allows you to use a digital item legally.

Can I use items for commercial projects?

Yes, depending on the license type you choose.
