Our Story

Teztecch is a Nagpur-based digital marketing and IT service provider, with sources indicating it was established in 2019 or 2021. Led by founder and CEO Tejaswwini Bhandarrkar, the agency specializes in website development,, SEO, social media management, and custom software, serving clients from Ayodhya Nagar, Nagpur, Maharashtra.

Today, we're proud to be trusted by over 500,000 customers worldwide, offering more than 15,000 premium themes and templates. Our community of creators continues to grow, bringing fresh ideas and innovative designs to the marketplace every day.
