How to Contact an Author?

If you have questions about a template, theme, or digital product purchased from TezTecch, you can contact the author directly for assistance. Authors are responsible for helping customers with product-related questions, technical issues, and guidance on using their items.

Before contacting an author, make sure you have your purchase details ready. This helps the author verify your purchase and provide faster support.

Most products available on TezTecch include item support from the author. You can reach the author through your account dashboard or through the support option available on the product page.

Steps to Contact an Author

  • Log in to your TezTecch account.
  • Go to your Purchases or Downloads section.
  • Select the product you purchased.
  • Click the Contact Author or Support button.
  • Write a message describing your issue or question.
  • Submit the request and wait for the author's response.

Tips for Faster Support

  • Include your purchase ID or order details.
  • Clearly explain the problem you are facing.
  • Attach screenshots if needed.
  • Mention the product version you are using.

Most authors respond within 24–48 hours. If you still need help, you can also contact the TezTecch Support Team through the Help Center.
