What is Item Support?

Item Support at TezTecch is a service provided to customers who purchase templates, themes, or digital products from the TezTecch marketplace. When you receive item support, our team is available to help you resolve issues or answer questions related to the item you purchased.

Item support ensures that your purchased template or theme works properly and that you can use all of its features correctly on your website.

Our support team helps customers with installation guidance, fixing technical bugs, and understanding how the product works. The goal of TezTecch support is to make sure you can successfully use your purchased item without problems.

What Item Support Includes

  • Help with installing templates or themes
  • Fixing bugs or technical issues
  • Guidance on using product features
  • Basic setup instructions

What Item Support Does Not Include

  • Custom development or design changes
  • Issues caused by third-party plugins
  • Website hosting or server configuration
  • Major customization requests

If you need help with an item purchased from TezTecch, you can contact the support team through the TezTecch Help Center. Make sure to provide your purchase details so the support team can assist you quickly.
