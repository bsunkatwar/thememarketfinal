Where Is My Purchase Code?

Your purchase code is a unique license code that you receive after buying a template, theme, or digital product from TezTecch. This code is used to verify your purchase and allows you to receive item support, updates, and access to product downloads.

The purchase code acts as proof that you have legally purchased the item from TezTecch and may be required when contacting the author or support team.

How to Find Your Purchase Code

  • Log in to your TezTecch account.
  • Go to your Downloads or Purchases section.
  • Find the product you purchased.
  • Click on Download or License Certificate.
  • Your Purchase Code will be listed inside the license document.

When You May Need Your Purchase Code

  • Requesting item support from the author
  • Activating a theme or plugin license
  • Verifying ownership of the product
  • Downloading updates or premium features

If you cannot find your purchase code, make sure you are logged into the correct TezTecch account used during the purchase. If the problem continues, contact the TezTecch Support Team for assistance.
