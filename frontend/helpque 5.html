Why Has My Item Been Removed?

Sometimes an item you purchased from TezTecch may no longer be available in the marketplace. This can happen for several reasons related to updates, policy compliance, or author decisions.

Even if an item has been removed from the marketplace, you can usually still download the item from your purchases section if you bought it before it was removed.

Common Reasons Why an Item May Be Removed

  • The author decided to remove the item from the marketplace.
  • The item no longer meets TezTecch quality or policy standards.
  • The product is outdated or no longer supported.
  • Legal or licensing issues required the item to be removed.

What Happens If You Already Purchased the Item?

  • You can still download the item from your TezTecch account.
  • Existing licenses remain valid.
  • Support availability depends on the author's support policy.

If you are unable to access your purchased item or have questions about a removed product, you can contact the TezTecch Support Team through the Help Center for further assistance.
