Cookie Policy

This policy explains how TezTecch uses cookies and similar tracking technologies on its websites.

What Cookies Are

Cookies are small data files created when you browse a website and stored on your device by your web browser.

Types of Cookies We Use

  • Necessary: Enable core website functionality.
  • Preferences: Remember your settings.
  • Statistics & Performance: Improve user experience.
  • Marketing: Show relevant ads.

Information Cookies Collect

  • Pages visited and actions taken
  • Browser and device information
  • Language and approximate location

Managing Cookie Preferences

You can manage or update your cookie preferences using the Cookie Settings option available in the footer.
